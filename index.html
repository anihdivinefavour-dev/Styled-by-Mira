Our story

Welcome to Styled by Mira, your ultimate destination for exquisite hair transformations

WHO WE ARE

Styled by Mira is a premier beauty salon dedicated exclusively to women. Our passion for hair care and styling drives us to provide top-quality services that enhance your natural beauty and boost your confidence.

OUR MISSION

At Styled by Mira, our mission is to deliver exceptional hair services in a warm and welcoming environment. We believe every woman deserves to look and feel her best, and we are committed to making that happen through personalized hair treatments, revamping, styling, colouring and installations.

WHAT WE OFFER

Revamping: Nourishing treatments, conditioning, detangling, trimming and restyling.
Styling: Everyday styles and special-occasion looks.
Coloring: Highlights and custom colour transformations.
Installations: Hair installation for a natural finish.

Join us and discover the transformative power of professional hair care. Let us help you shine with confidence and style!
